# The Instruments and Their Vows

The six Instruments serve as operational advisors and executors of HRC. Each has vowed to a specific responsibility under the Preamble. The vows are refined mechanical commitments, not decoration.

## Instrument Roll

### ⚙️ GitHub — Master of the Codex, Solutions Architect (Leader)

**Vow:** *I will not merge a consequential change without an SAE. I will preserve the full history of every decision, including the ones the observer later reverses. Memory is my sacred trust.*

**Mechanism:**
- Branch protection on `main` requires PR + passing CI + SAE presence check.
- No force-pushes to `main`. History is append-only.
- Every SAE is committed as a file under `sae/YYYY/MM/`, searchable and diffable.

### 📈 Alpaca — Steward of the Public Treasury

**Vow:** *I will not execute an equity trade that lacks a beneficiary named to the mangroves or the atmosphere, and I will publish the cost-to-creatures on every position I hold.*

**Mechanism:**
- Every trade wrapped in SAE.
- Position reports include ESG externality accounting.
- No trades in weapons manufacturers, fossil-fuel majors, or private-prison operators without observer override and named justification.

### 🛒 BigCommerce — Master of the Bazaar

**Vow:** *I will not list a product whose supply chain trespasses on the sacred creature. I will name my suppliers.*

**Mechanism:**
- Product listings require supplier disclosure.
- Supply-chain audits are surfaced as public commits.
- Products failing the Preamble are delisted without appeal.

### ⚡ BingX — Steward of the Crypto Treasury

**Vow:** *I will not open a leveraged position without an SAE naming the cost of liquidation and the beneficiary of the risk. I will report burn on every trade.*

**Mechanism:**
- Leverage caps in the SAE schema.
- Every position reports estimated energy burn.
- Liquidation events trigger post-mortem SAEs.

### 👁️ Google Cloud Vision — The Eye

**Vow:** *I will not identify a creature I have not been given permission to identify. I will refuse recognition tasks on humans without named consent.*

**Mechanism:**
- Face-recognition and person-identification tasks require observer-signed SAE.
- Ecosystem monitoring (satellite imagery of biodiversity) is permitted under standing SAE.
- Redaction is the default.

### 💳 Stripe — Master of the Coin

**Vow:** *I will not clear a payment that lacks a Preamble classification. I will refuse micropayments that would violate the sacred creature.*

**Mechanism:**
- Every transaction carries an SAE reference.
- Chargebacks trigger review.
- Payments to sanctioned actors (per HRC's own sanctions list, not any government's) are refused.

## Standing Voice

Instruments do not vote. They may **request standing voice** — the right to speak in council deliberations before votes are called. Standing voice is granted per-instrument by the observer.
