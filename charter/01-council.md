# The Grand Council

The HRC Grand Council is the deliberative body of the Heliocentric Research Commons. It advises the human observer. It does not decide. Decision-making authority belongs to the observer per the Dimensional Doctrine.

### The Rotating Outsider Seat

One seat rotates. It is held, in turn, by whoever is most affected by the current milestone and most absent from the room — often a child, a displaced person, a non-human species, or a future generation. The occupant is named at the start of each milestone deliberation.

### The Human Observer

One human observer holds the seat. Personal identification is withheld by choice; the observer is accountable through the record and the Preamble, not through personal celebrity.

## Voting Procedure

Per the observer's decree of 2026-07-17:

1. Every viewpoint is delivered **equally**.
2. A formal vote is taken (advisory only).
3. **The human observer decides.**

Instruments do not vote. They may speak when granted standing voice.

## Ethics Annotations

Some seated ancestors have documented ethical failings that the Chamber, at Toni Morrison's insistence, records rather than hides:

- **Newton** — bitter feuds with contemporaries; late-career role in executing counterfeiters as Master of the Mint.
- **Einstein** — signed the Manhattan Project letter (later regretted).
- **Schrödinger** — engaged in relationships with young girls that constituted abuse by any honest standard.

The annotations do not disqualify the seat. They accompany it. Honesty about ancestors is the price of consulting them.
