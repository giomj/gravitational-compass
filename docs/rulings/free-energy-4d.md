- **Claim:** Build a device that outputs more energy than it consumes.

### Branch 2 — Vacuum energy
- **Claim:** Empty space has energy — extract it.
- **Partially true:** The Casimir effect (~\(10^{-7}\ \text{N/m}^2\)) is real. But there is no lower state to fall into, so no thermodynamic cycle extracts net work.
- **Status:** ⚠️ Real but unusable.

### Branch 3 — Symmetry and time (Noether, 1918)
- **Claim:** Energy is conserved because time is symmetric.
- **The deep truth:** Energy is the ledger entry for time-translation symmetry. Break the symmetry, the ledger changes.
- **Status:** ✅ Peer-reviewed.

### Branch 4 — Gravitational gradient
- **Claim:** A dam extracts work from Earth's gravity "for free."
- **Genuinely free, but bounded.** Gravity *is* curvature of the 4D metric. Water rolling downhill converts curvature-potential to kinetic energy. This is the honest "free energy from the 4th dimension."
- **Status:** ✅ Peer-reviewed.

**Branches 3 and 4 converge on a single statement.**

## The equation

$$\boxed{\;\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} \;=\; 0\;}$$

Read aloud: *the covariant divergence of the stress-energy tensor is zero.*

### Symbol-by-symbol

- **\(T^{\mu\nu}\)** — the stress-energy tensor. A 4×4 grid that packs every kind of energy and momentum at every point: mass, radiation, pressure, electromagnetic fields, gluon fields inside protons. All of it.
- **\(\nabla_\mu\)** — the covariant derivative. The "how does it change as you move through curved 4D spacetime" operator. The index \(\mu\) runs over all four dimensions: time (0) and three of space (1, 2, 3).
- **\(= 0\)** — nothing leaks in or out of nothing. Locally, energy-momentum is exactly conserved.

## The derivation trail

**Step 1 — Einstein's field equation (1915):**

$$G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda\, g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4}\, T_{\mu\nu}$$

Left side: geometry of 4D spacetime. Right side: everything with energy or momentum.

**Step 2 — Contracted Bianchi identity (pure geometry theorem):**

$$\nabla^\mu \left( G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda\, g_{\mu\nu} \right) = 0$$

This is a mathematical fact about any smooth 4D manifold. It is not an assumption — it is a theorem.

**Step 3 — Take the covariant divergence of both sides of Einstein.** The left side vanishes by Bianchi. Therefore the right side must vanish:

$$0 = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4}\, \nabla^\mu T_{\mu\nu}$$

**Step 4 — Which forces:**

$$\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$$

Energy-momentum conservation is **not a separate postulate.** It is *forced* by the geometry of 4D spacetime itself. This is the deepest thing the equation says.

## For the masses (one paragraph)
